Delete Account

Last updated: March 10, 2026

How to request deletion

Send an email to contactanos@circulo-app.org from the same email address used in your Circulo account with subject: Account Deletion Request.

Information to include in the request

  • Registered account email.
  • Optional: reason for deletion (for support and quality purposes).

Data that will be deleted

  • Your account profile data (name, email, profile picture URL, internal user ID).
  • Your memberships and participation links in communities and events.
  • Your authentication-related state linked to your account.

Data that may be retained

  • Security and audit logs required for fraud prevention and platform protection.
  • Data required by applicable legal or regulatory obligations.

Retained data is limited to what is strictly necessary and kept only for the legally required period or security necessity.

Processing time

We aim to process deletion requests as soon as reasonably possible. Timing may vary depending on legal/security checks and system backups.
